space-xs: 0.25rem space-sm: 0.5rem space-md: 1rem space-lg: 1.5rem space-xl: 2rem space-2xl: 3rem space-3xl: 4.5rem --- ## Brand & Style This design system expresses a vibrant, contemporary grassroots commerce identity rooted in warm, tactile craftsmanship, authentic rural enterprise, and artisanal luxury. It bridges traditional Indian heritage with modern e-commerce utility. ### Brand Personality & Emotional Voice - **Grounded & Artisanal:** Celebrates natural textures, rich soil, handmade artifacts, and slow commerce. - **Empowered & Trustworthy:** Dignified, transparent pricing and clear merchant attribution that honor rural makers and regional producers. - **Warm Tactility:** Replaces sterile tech-minimalism with rich, earthen tones, sculpted surfaces, and expressive typographic personality. ### Design Movement: Modern Earthy Tactile The system mixes warm editorial design with tactile physical metaphors. Surfaces draw inspiration from sun-baked terracotta, raw unbleached cotton, and dense forest canopy foliage. Visual hierarchy relies on tonal layering, rich contrast borders, and pill-shaped touch surfaces rather than cold, ungrounded blurs or heavy drop shadows. ## Colors The palette balances sun-dried clay with lush forest foliage and unbleached cotton paper surfaces. ### Semantic Palette Architecture - **Primary (`#BC6C25` - Terracotta Clay):** Expresses raw craftsmanship, action focal points, primary CTAs, active states, and artisan highlights. - **Secondary (`#1B4332` - Deep Forest Foliage):** Grounded institutional weight, secondary buttons, dense headers, structural anchors, and organic certification badges. - **Tertiary (`#DDA15E` - Harvest Gold):** Warm harvest accents, ratings, special badges, seasonal festival notifications, and subtle container fills. - **Neutral (`#1C2520` - Charcoal Forest):** High-contrast, deeply human reading charcoal, carrying natural green undertones for long-form readability without harshness. ### Supporting Canvas & Background Tones - **Base Canvas (`#FBF9F1`):** A warm, sunlit cotton cream that serves as the root app canvas. - **Surface Elevation 1 (`#F5F1E8`):** Unbleached parchment cream used for structural card frames and grouped module backgrounds. - **Surface Elevation 2 (`#EDE6D6`):** Deeper clay-stone tint for inner wells, inputs, and chip surfaces. - **Border Subtle (`rgba(28, 37, 32, 0.08)`):** Quiet edge definition keeping tactile surfaces crisp without visual noise. ## Typography The typography pairs the expressive, hand-cut character dynamics of Bricolage Grotesque with the universal multi-script clarity of Noto Sans (offering native parity across Latin and Devanagari Hindi typography). ### Typographic Roles & Execution - **Headlines (Bricolage Grotesque):** Used for marketing heroes, product collection titles, price totals, and artisan spotlights. The subtle rhythmic eccentricities reflect carved wooden block prints and hand-cut signage. - **Body & Labels (Noto Sans):** Provides neutral, highly legible copy for product descriptions, multi-tiered provenance receipts, nutrition facts, and Hindi translations. Ensures consistent metrics across Latin and Devanagari glyphs. - **Pacing & Optical Kerning:** Headlines employ negative tracking to ensure sturdy visual impact, while small caps and badge labels feature positive letter spacing for quick verification in commerce streams. ## Layout & Spacing This design system uses a flexible 12-column grid system tuned for fluid mobile-first shopping experiences and curated editorial desktop lookbooks. ### Grid & Breakpoints - **Mobile (<640px):** 4-column layout with a 1rem margin and 1rem gutter. Full-width sticky checkout bars and horizontal scroll rails for artisanal producer showcases. - **Tablet (640px–1024px):** 8-column layout with 2rem margins and 1.5rem gutters. Product feeds adjust to 2 or 3 cards per row. - **Desktop (>1024px):** 12-column layout capped at 1280px maximum content width with 3.5rem outer gutters. Split-screen product displays anchor high-detail imagery alongside artisan provenance cards. ### Spacing Principles Component density respects generous touch margins (minimum 48px touch targets for rural connectivity and varied device conditions). Spacing scales uniformly on an 8pt base grid (`space-sm` at 8px, `space-md` at 16px, `space-lg` at 24px). ## Elevation & Depth Visual hierarchy uses warm, physical tonal layering paired with soft, terracotta-tinted ambient shadows. Sterile grey drops are avoided in favor of warm, sunlight-cast depth. ### Depth Architecture - **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** `#FBF9F1` (Sunlit Cotton). - **Level 1 (Resting Cards & Surfaces):** `#F5F1E8` framed by a subtle perimeter outline (`rgba(28, 37, 32, 0.06)`). Shadow: `0 2px 8px rgba(188, 108, 37, 0.04)`. - **Level 2 (Interactive Cards Hover / Flyouts):** Lifted with dual shadows: a 1px border shift to harvest gold, a contact shadow of `0 4px 12px rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.06)`, and an ambient bloom of `0 12px 24px rgba(188, 108, 37, 0.08)`. - **Level 3 (Modals, Drawers & Cart Sheets):** Elevated using `0 20px 40px rgba(28, 37, 32, 0.16)` with a backdrop scrim washed in deep forest green at 35% opacity (`rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.35)`). ## Shapes The shape system adopts a roundedness level of `3` (Pill-shaped & Generous Curves), referencing hand-turned clay pottery, polished river stones, and smooth wooden weaving shuttles. ### Geometric Strategy - **Buttons, Badges & Chips:** Full pill geometry (`border-radius: 9999px` or minimum `1rem`) for approachable, tactile touch targets. - **Product & Merchant Cards:** Generous 1.5rem to 2rem corner curves (`rounded-lg`), creating an inviting, organic framing for artisan goods. - **Modals, Floating Sheets & Hero Panels:** Sweeping 2.5rem to 3rem top radiuses (`rounded-xl`), creating a fluid, welcoming transition between navigation layers. ## Components ### Buttons - **Primary:** Full pill border radius, solid Terracotta Clay (`#BC6C25`) background with crisp `#FBF9F1` typography. Hover state shifts smoothly to `#A2581A` with a micro-scale transition (`scale: 1.02`). Active state presses to `scale: 0.98`. - **Secondary:** Deep Forest Green (`#1B4332`) with `#FBF9F1` text for authoritative actions (e.g., "Meet the Artisan", "Track Order"). - **Outline / Ghost:** `#F5F1E8` surface with a 1.5px solid border in `#1B4332`, text set in `#1B4332`. Hover fills with a delicate wash of `#DDA15E` at 15% opacity. ### Chips & Badges - **Provenance & Organic Tags:** Pill-shaped, composed of Deep Forest Green tint backgrounds (`rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.08)`) with `#1B4332` bold micro-labels, preceded by a miniature hand-crafted emblem. - **Filter Chips:** Parchment surface (`#F5F1E8`) with subtle border; when toggled active, shifts to solid Terracotta (`#BC6C25`) with light text and an animated icon transition. ### Cards - **Artisan & Product Cards:** Built on generous 2rem corner curves. Surfaces feature rich photography with a warm 2% amber gradient wash. Card bodies include dual-row metadata: price in bold Bricolage Grotesque, harvest or craft cluster origin in Noto Sans small caps, and an embedded quick-add pill trigger. - **Impact & Provenance Cards:** Tinted in light harvest gold (`rgba(221, 161, 94, 0.12)`) featuring verified artisan cooperative signatures, geographical GI-tag indicators, and transparent farmer payout breakdowns. ### Form Inputs & Checkboxes - **Input Fields:** Generous 1rem curved input wells (`#F5F1E8`) with an inset warm shadow and a 1.5px border (`rgba(28, 37, 32, 0.12)`). On focus, transition border color to `#BC6C25` with an outer terracotta halo glow (`0 0 0 3px rgba(188, 108, 37, 0.2)`). - **Checkboxes & Radios:** Soft-pill rounded boxes with 2px borders in `#1B4332`. Selected states fill with `#BC6C25` and display an ivory check glyph. ### Lists & Navigation - **Artisan Directory Lists:** Separated by soft earthen hairline dividers (`rgba(28, 37, 32, 0.06)`). Each item features a thumbnail avatar, village origin badge, and primary craft indicator with smooth right-chevron indicators. - **Bottom Navigation Bar (Mobile):** Floating pill dock, elevated with Level 2 warm shadow, housing localized Hindi/English dual-language label toggles and high-contrast thumb-zone actions.
